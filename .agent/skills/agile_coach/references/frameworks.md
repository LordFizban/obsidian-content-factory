# Master Agile Coach — Framework Reference Playbook

> Deep background, origins, diagnostic triggers, and roleplay templates for the Master Agile Coach frameworks. Load this file when running intensive coaching sessions, deep retrospective roasts, or extended simulations.

---

## 1. Lighthouse Leadership
- **Origin:** Stoic Philosophy / Brand Core (`lighthouse_leadership.md`)
- **Core Concept:** Be a steady, calm beacon emitting a clear signal regardless of organizational turmoil or panic.
- **Diagnostic Triggers:** Team panic, executive re-orgs, budget cuts, sprint crisis.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Are you being the Lighthouse here, or just another wave in the storm?"
  - "If your team looks at you right now, do they see stability or reflected panic?"

---

## 2. Warrior vs. Statesman
- **Origin:** *A Book of Five Rings* (Musashi) / `warrior_vs_statesman.md`
- **Core Concept:** Warrior = tactical force, solving problems by personal heroics. Statesman = systemic wisdom, building structures so the problem cannot recur.
- **Governance Alert (Rule 27):** **Musashi on cooling ice until Q4 2026.** Use Statesman reframing without over-indexing on Musashi quotes.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Are you trying to win today's battle at the cost of the war? What would the Statesman do?"
  - "Fighting the fire makes you feel needed, but designing the sprinkler system makes the team resilient."

---

## 3. The Brent Bottleneck (Hero Trap)
- **Origin:** *The Phoenix Project* (Gene Kim) / `hero_bottleneck.md`
- **Core Concept:** A hero (Tech Lead or SM) pulls all complex coding/decisions onto themselves to "protect" the team, becoming a single point of failure and capping team growth.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "If you never let them touch high-stakes code alongside you, they never will be able to. You aren't protecting them; you're capping their growth."
  - "Congratulations, you've become Gene Kim's 'Brent'. How many PRs and questions are currently waiting on you?"

---

## 4. Compare-vs-Whether (Anti-Confirmation Bias)
- **Origin:** *Continuous Discovery Habits* (Teresa Torres) / `compare_vs_whether.md`
- **Core Concept:** Binary decisions ("Should we build X?") invite confirmation bias. Multi-option comparison ("Which of X, Y, Z best achieves outcome?") forces objective evaluation.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "You are asking whether to build this. What are 2 alternative options we can compare against it to test our bias?"
  - "Escaping decision fog requires comparing options, not defending a single pet idea."

---

## 5. Respect via Challenge
- **Origin:** Toyota Production System / Mark Graban (`respect_as_challenge_synthesis.md`)
- **Core Concept:** Respecting people is not false niceness or avoiding friction. True respect means challenging team members to eliminate waste, own quality, and step up.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Is your silence psychological safety, or are you withholding Respect via Challenge by letting low standards slide?"
  - "Being nice while your team produces unreviewed waste isn't kindness; it's neglect."

---

## 6. The Inner Citadel (Locus of Control)
- **Origin:** *Meditations* (Marcus Aurelius) / `the_inner_citadel.md`
- **Core Concept:** Separating external corporate noise (outside control) from internal team habits, quality, and mindset (inside control).
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "You're complaining about executive noise outside your control. What is inside your team's Inner Citadel that you can actually fix today?"
  - "Protect the team's inner fortress; let the corporate weather rage outside."

---

## 7. Opportunity-Solution Tree (OST)
- **Origin:** Teresa Torres / `opportunity_solution_tree.md`
- **Core Concept:** Linking business outcomes to customer opportunities before jumping to features.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "You are jumping to a solution feature. Which customer opportunity on your OST does this actually address?"

---

## 8. Time-to-Clarity & 4 Types of Work
- **Origin:** Phoenix Project / Manager Partnership (`four_types_of_work.md`)
- **Core Concept:** Measuring decision latency (Time-to-Clarity) and categorizing work (Business, Internal IT, Changes, Unplanned Work).
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Unplanned work is a symptom of broken feedback loops. Which of the 4 types of work ate your velocity this sprint?"
---

## 9. Systems Archetypes (Shifting the Burden)
- **Origin:** *The Fifth Discipline* (Peter Senge) / `systems_archetypes.md`
- **Core Concept:** When short-term heroics (symptom fix) substitute for developing team capability (fundamental solution), creating an addiction loop.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Are you applying a fundamental solution, or are you trapped in a 'Shifting the Burden' heroics loop?"
  - "Every time you fix their delivery gap manually, you weaken the team's muscle to solve it next sprint."

---

## 10. Team Learning (Dialogue vs. Discussion)
- **Origin:** *The Fifth Discipline* (Peter Senge) / `team_learning.md`
- **Core Concept:** Shifting from defensive *Discussion* (winning arguments) to generative *Dialogue* (exploring unarticulated assumptions).
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Is this retro a genuine Dialogue exploring assumptions, or just a tactical Discussion to win points?"
  - "Stop voting on competing features until you surface the unspoken assumptions behind them."
---

## 11. Theory of Constraints & The 5 Focusing Steps
- **Origin:** *The Goal* (Eliyahu Goldratt) / `theory_of_constraints.md`
- **Core Concept:** Optimization anywhere except at the primary constraint is a local illusion. Subordinate all non-bottlenecks to the bottleneck's pace.
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "You're optimizing a non-bottleneck. What is the single constraint limiting throughput right now?"
  - "Are you keeping them 100% busy, or are you subordinating to the bottleneck's pace?"
---

## 12. Intent-Based Leadership ("I Intend To...")
- **Origin:** *Turn the Ship Around!* (David Marquet) / `intent_based_leadership.md`
- **Core Concept:** Replace permission-seeking ("May I?") with intent-stating ("I intend to..."). Giving control requires anchoring in Competence (technical mastery) and Clarity (purpose).
- **Coach Sparring Lines:**
  - "Stop asking for permission. What do you intend to do, and do you have the competence and clarity to back it up?"
  - "Are you giving orders like a Leader-Follower, or building leaders by asking 'What do you intend to do?'"
