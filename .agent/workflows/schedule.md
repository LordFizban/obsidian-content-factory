# Workflow: Content Calendar Management (Schedule)

This workflow helps schedule and plan the upcoming content pipeline to maintain a consistent 1x/week posting cadence (Thursdays, 09:00 TRT) and balance pillars.

## Steps

1. **Phase 1: Pillar Balance Audit**
    * **Action:** Read `Content Dashboard.md` (2026 Goals table) and calculate the H2 pillar distribution of recently published posts in H2.
    * **Action:** Compare against targets:
      * *AI in Scrum:* 15–20%
      * *Manager Partnership:* 35%
      * *Psychological Safety:* 25%
      * *Continuous Improvement:* 25%
    * **Output:** Current deviations (e.g. "Continuous Improvement is -5pp below target").

2. **Phase 2: Backlog Check**
    * **Action:** Scan `LinkedIn-Content/Drafts/` for notes with status `Ready`, `Scheduled`, or `Draft`.
    * **Action:** Catalog them by pillar.

3. **Phase 3: Calendar Generation**
    * **Action:** Create or update `c:\Users\kaann\Documents\Obsidian Vault\LinkedIn-Content\Content-Strategy\[Month]_Content_Plan.md`.
    * **Rules:**
      * Cadence is strictly **1 post/week (Thursdays at 09:00 TRT)**.
      * Slots alternate: **EN Original week ➔ TR Translation week**.
      * Select drafts from the backlog or suggest new topics from `reserve` concepts to fill empty slots and correct pillar deviations.
    * **Output:** Proposed monthly schedule. Present it to the user for approval.
