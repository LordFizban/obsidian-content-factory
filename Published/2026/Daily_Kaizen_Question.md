## 📝 Content

Tuesday I wrote about stealing Kaizen from Lean.

Danish Soomro pushed back:
"Retros have become a ceremony that takes hours away from my workweek."

He's not wrong.

If a retro feels like time wasted — it was a bad retro.
But here's the question I keep coming back to:

What if the problem isn't the retro itself,
but waiting 3 weeks to have one?

A 5-minute daily "What's broken today?" costs almost nothing.
A 3-week feedback gap costs everything you forgot to fix.

💬 What's the smallest improvement ritual that actually stuck on your team?
