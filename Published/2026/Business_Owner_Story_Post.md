# Business Owner Story Post

**Published:** Thursday, February 27, 2026
**Pillar:** Manager Partnership
**Format:** Text Post (no visual)

---

## Post Text

The team marked it done. The Business Owner said it wasn't.

Last week I wrote about a vendor dependency that cost us five days of tester capacity. But the story didn't end there.

After the dust settled, I sat down with the Business Owner. Not to report. To listen.

The team had completed the sprint item. Tests were executed. From their side: delivered.

From the Business Owner's side: not delivered.

Her definition of success wasn't tests completed. It was test results positive. In her eyes, it wasn't done.

Same sprint item. Two definitions of success. Nobody had checked.

I asked one question: What would success have looked like for you?

What she described was clear. And completely absent from our acceptance criteria.

We agreed to take it to the Product Owner together.

She committed to participating in acceptance criteria definition going forward. Not because I told her to. Because she saw the gap herself.

This wasn't a delivery problem. It was a Definition of Done problem.

When did you last discover that done meant something different to the person who asked for it?
