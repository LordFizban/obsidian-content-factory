### 📝 Post Summary

This post introduced the concept of AI as a "sparring partner" in Scrum, not a replacement for human judgment. It referenced **StoryReady**, a lightweight tool built using Google's Antigravity AI to validate user stories against INVEST criteria before sprint planning.

**Key themes:**

- AI as assistant, not replacement — supports thinking, doesn't replace it
