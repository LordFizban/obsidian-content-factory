## 📝 Caption

I used to think being a Scrum Master meant "shielding" the team.
Fighting every fire. Stopping every interruption.
But you can't stop a storm.

Now, I think of it more like being a Lighthouse.

When the timeline slips and the pressure mounts, I don't try to hold back the waves.
I just make sure I'm standing firm.
Visible. Consistent.
Keeping the light on so the team can find their own way through.

A panic-free leader is the loudest signal in a noisy room.

Are you shielding your team from reality, or simply helping them navigate it?
