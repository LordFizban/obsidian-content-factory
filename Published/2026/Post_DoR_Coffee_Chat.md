# The Power of a United Front

**Headline:** The most effective Jira workflow wasn't built in Jira. It was built in a 15-minute video call.

**Visual:** `dor_quote_tile.png` (Attached)

**Body:**
My team was struggling with vague requirements. We defined a strict Definition of Ready (DoR), but "process enforcement" often feels like "bureaucracy" to stakeholders.

So we didn't send a PDF. My Product Owner and I asked the Business Owner for a virtual coffee.

**The Strategy: A United Front**
The PO represented the "What" (Business Value). I represented the "How" (Flow).

We didn't talk about the Scrum Guide or gatekeeping. We spoke her language:
"Every hour we spend guessing what a request means is an hour we aren't building it. If we agree on the 'Input Criteria' (DoR) upfront, our 'Output Velocity' goes up."

She paused. "So, better inputs = faster delivery?"

"Exactly."

She didn't just agree. She mandated it.
"Note to all stakeholders: All future requests must meet these criteria before submission."

**The Lesson:**
If you want to change the system, don't hide behind the process guide.

1. Partner with your PO (don't go alone).
2. Explain the *business cost* of the ambiguity.
3. Let the manager be the champion.
