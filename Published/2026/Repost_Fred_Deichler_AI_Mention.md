## 📝 Commentary Text

Got mentioned in a conversation about AI adoption and Scrum Masters.

What caught my attention wasn't the mention itself. It was realizing how far the team has come since that first experiment.

It started with me using AI as a "Red Team" during refinement. Finding gaps the team would have shipped. One developer picked it up the next sprint. No one asked him to.

This week, the team demoed AI-assisted User Story breakdown at Sprint Review. Risk profiles, complexity scoring, INVEST criteria. They built it themselves.

I didn't implement anything. I showed one artifact that solved a real problem. The team did the rest.

That's what adoption looks like when you treat it as Product Discovery instead of a training program: belief follows demonstrable increments.
