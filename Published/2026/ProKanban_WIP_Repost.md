## 📝 Repost Commentary

This is the "why" behind the notebook I started keeping.

I tracked every blocker for 3 sprints. Not velocity. Not story points. Just: what are we waiting for, and how long?

The pattern was exactly what ProKanban describes here — talented people, working hard, everything "in progress," nothing done.

The invisible culprit wasn't skill or effort.
It was too much work in flight with no visibility into where the silence was.

We didn't need a new tool.
We needed to stop starting and start finishing.
