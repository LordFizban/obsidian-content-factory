# Eliyahu M. Goldratt

## Summary
Eliyahu M. Goldratt (1947–2011) was an Israeli business management guru and physicist who created the Theory of Constraints (TOC). Author of the seminal business novel *The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement* (1984/2004), which introduced TOC, the 5 Focusing Steps, and the Herbie metaphor.

## Relevance
- Key strategic source for **Book-Source Authority Borrowing (Rule 26)** — Goldratt is the intellectual ancestor of Gene Kim (*The Phoenix Project*) and modern DevOps/Lean flow.
- Directly supports the **Continuous Improvement (20%)** and **Manager Partnership (35%)** content pillars.
- Provides the foundational framework for bottleneck identification, throughput accounting, and eliminating non-bottleneck overproduction in software development.
