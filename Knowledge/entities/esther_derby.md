# Esther Derby

## Summary

Esther Derby is a globally recognized expert in change leadership, retrospectives, and organizational dynamics. Author of "Agile Retrospectives: Making Good Teams Great" (with Diana Larsen). Known for systems-thinking approach to leadership and team autonomy.

## Key Concepts Referenced

- **Dependency Dynamic:** Teams don't start dependent; leaders manufacture dependency by jumping in when teams hesitate. Adults who manage mortgages aren't inherently helpless — they are responding to the system.
- **Constraints that Bound Autonomy:** Managers must provide clarity about goals, necessary conditions, and establish constraints that bound autonomy. Without these, teams flail. There's territory between micro-management and turning a team loose.
- **Defense of Managers:** Most managers aren't taught how to manage the space between control and autonomy in their professional education or new manager training.
