# Peter Senge

## Summary
Peter Senge is a senior lecturer at MIT Sloan School of Management and founder of the Society for Organizational Learning (SoL). He is the author of *The Fifth Discipline: The Art & Practice of The Learning Organization* (1990/1994), which popularized Systems Thinking as the core discipline that integrates organizational learning.
