# Chris Argyris

> **Chris Argyris** (1923–2013) was a professor at Harvard Business School and one of the founding fathers of organizational learning. Known for **Model I vs. Model II Theory**, **Defensive Routines**, **Double-Loop Learning**, and the **Ladder of Inference** (co-developed with Donald Schön).

---

## Strategic Significance

Argyris provides the diagnostic psychological bridge for why intelligent leaders resist Agile transformation and system-level feedback:
1. **Model I Theory (Defensive Routines):** Leaders default to unilateral control, winning exchanges, and suppressing negative feedback under threat.
2. **Espoused Theory vs. Theory-in-Use:** Leaders talk about agility, collaboration, and psychological safety, but their behavior under pressure defends territory.
3. **Double-Loop Learning:** True systemic improvement requires questioning underlying assumptions and governing variables, not just optimizing operational tasks.
