# Team Learning

## Definition
The discipline of aligning and developing the capacity of a team to create the results its members truly desire. It builds on personal mastery and shared vision, centered on the distinction between *Dialogue* (free-flowing exploration of underlying assumptions) and *Discussion* (defending conflicting views to win an argument).

## Current Rule
**Rule 34 (Proposed):** Frame sprint retrospectives as *Dialogue* sessions rather than *Discussion* debates. If a team enters defensive discussion mode, the Scrum Master should pause the meeting and inquire into underlying assumptions rather than taking votes on competing features.

## Evolution
- **2026-08-02:** Ingested from Peter Senge's *The Fifth Discipline*. Designated as a key focus area for Psychological Safety rituals and facilitation content.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Pure dialogue can feel slow or unfocused to action-oriented engineering teams; over-emphasizing dialogue risks turning retrospectives into unproductive therapy sessions.
2. **Data Gap:** Need analytics data on whether posts focusing on dialogue vs discussion in Scrum retrospectives drive higher comments or profile views.
3. **Bias Check:** We must avoid assuming every team argument is a failure of dialogue; sometimes healthy, energetic technical debates (discussion) are necessary to resolve concrete architectural choices quickly.
