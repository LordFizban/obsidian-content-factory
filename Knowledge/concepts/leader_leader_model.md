# Leader-Leader Model

## Definition
A governance paradigm contrasting with the top-down Leader-Follower model. It pushes decision-making authority to where information sits, creating active problem-solvers at every level rather than compliant order-takers.

## Current Rule
**Rule 39 (Proposed):** Evaluate team health by decision distribution: if all technical and priority decisions funnel through the SM or Tech Lead, the team is in Leader-Follower failure mode.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Without central coordination, decentralized leader-leader systems can suffer from architectural drift and duplicate effort across squads.
2. **Data Gap:** Need analytics data on whether leader-leader posts generate more saves than leader-follower critiques.
3. **Bias Check:** We must not confuse leader-leader empowerment with abdication of leadership responsibility during actual crises.
