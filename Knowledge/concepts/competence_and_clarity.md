# Competence and Clarity (Pillars of Control)

## Definition
The two non-negotiable prerequisites for giving up control. Relinquishing control without **Competence** (technical mastery) leads to errors; relinquishing control without **Clarity** (alignment with organizational purpose) leads to chaos.

## Current Rule
**Rule 40 (Proposed):** Before delegating sprint scope or architecture control to a team, audit both Competence (Do they have technical skills/DoD?) and Clarity (Do they understand the customer outcome?). Never delegate into a void.

## Evolution
- **2026-08-02:** Ingested from *Turn the Ship Around!*.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Using "lack of competence" as a reason to hold back control can become an excuse for micromanagers to never delegate.
2. **Data Gap:** Need performance data on posts exploring the Competence vs Clarity matrix.
3. **Bias Check:** Ensure clarity is co-created with the team rather than dictated top-down.
