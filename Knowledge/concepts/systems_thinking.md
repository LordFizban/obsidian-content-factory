# Systems Thinking

## Definition
The discipline of seeing wholes, recognizing interrelationships rather than linear cause-effect chains, and seeing patterns of change rather than static snapshots. It posits that organizational behavior is driven by underlying structural relationships rather than individual motives or isolated events.

## Current Rule
**Rule 32 (Proposed):** Frame team blockers as structural feedback loops rather than individual performance issues. When introducing a process change, identify the compensating feedback loop before implementation to prevent systemic pushback.

## Evolution
- **2026-08-02:** Ingested from Peter Senge's *The Fifth Discipline*. Established as the primary theoretical anchor for the Statesman stance in the Warrior vs. Statesman framework.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Systems thinking can induce analysis paralysis in fast-moving teams; sometimes a simple, local linear fix is sufficient and faster than mapping complex feedback loops.
2. **Data Gap:** We need data on whether posts referencing formal systems thinking concepts (e.g., feedback loops, leverage points) outperform simple operational stories in reach or save rates on LinkedIn.
3. **Bias Check:** We may be over-attributing team issues to systemic structures and underestimating individual skill gaps or lack of accountability.
