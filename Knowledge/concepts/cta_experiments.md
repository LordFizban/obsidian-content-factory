# CTA Experiments

## Definition

Systematic testing of different Call-to-Action formats at the end of LinkedIn posts to optimize for meaningful comments (weighted ~12x a like under [[360_brew]]). Q1 baseline: avg 0.5 comments per post. Target: ≥1.0 avg.

## Current Rule

- Explicit, scenario-based questions outperform open-ended CTAs.
- **Never use fill-in-the-blank CTAs** (Rule 21 ✅ Confirmed).
- Manager Partnership posts specifically need stronger CTAs (Q1: 0 comments avg on MP content; W20 showed some improvement with 1 comment on Org Chart).
- Save-worthy insights at the end can substitute for explicit CTAs if the content naturally invites bookmarking (proven by the W22 saves spike).

## Evolution

- **Jan 2026:** No systematic CTA testing. Mixed results.
- **Feb 2026:** Noticed Manager Partnership content generates 0 comments regardless of CTA.
- **Mar 2026:** Identified that scenario-based questions outperform open-ended CTAs (Q1 Lessons).
- **Apr 2026:** Three-format A/B test planned (explicit question, fill-in-blank, contrast question). Fill-in-the-blank format failed spectacularly (0 comments, Rule 21 confirmed).
- **May 2026:** Scenario-based CTAs on Calm Mind (1 comment) and Org Chart (1 comment) validated. However, comments still do not correlate strictly with CTA format or impression volume — even 660 impressions generated 0 comments on Perception Is Strong. Localized Turkish posts stacked during travel mode generated 0 comments but 17k+ impressions, proving reach can be isolated from dialogue.
- **Jun 2026:** Experiment 1 validated. The save-worthy exercise CTA was a massive success for Sprint That Ate Itself (7 saves at 4,430 imps). However, low-reach posts under 300 imps (Inner Citadel, Favorite Weapon, Bottleneck) generated 0 saves, suggesting a minimum reach threshold (e.g. 500-1000 imps) is still required to generate saves even with optimized CTAs.

## Open Questions

1. Is the 0-comment problem on English MP content a CTA problem or a topic problem? (MP may inherently be less comment-worthy than AI content, though scenario CTAs show early viability.)
2. Does ending with a question reduce Save rate? (Saves appear to correlate with scale and value-density rather than the specific ending sentence.)
3. Should we test Turkish-localized posts with explicit Turkish CTAs in Q3 to see if we can trigger conversations in Turkish?

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps

1. **Against:** Engagement CTAs can be perceived as transactional and damage long-term trust/authenticity. Audiences may develop banner-blindness to structured save prompts.
2. **Data Gap:** Longitudinal save rate tracking comparing posts with implicit save-worthy value vs. posts with explicit save prompts.
3. **Bias Check:** Social media best practices are written by influencers whose audience dynamics differ significantly from senior B2B corporate decision-makers.
