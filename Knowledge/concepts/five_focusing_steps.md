# 5 Focusing Steps of TOC

## Definition
Goldratt's 5-step continuous improvement loop:
1. **Identify** the system's constraint.
2. **Exploit** the constraint (maximize output with existing resources).
3. **Subordinate** everything else to the above decision (align non-bottlenecks to feed the constraint).
4. **Elevate** the constraint (invest to increase capacity).
5. **Repeat** (go back to step 1; prevent inertia from becoming the new constraint).

## Current Rule
**Rule 36 (Proposed):** Subordinate non-bottleneck team capacity to the bottleneck's pace. If testing is the bottleneck, developers should assist QA or slow down feature creation rather than building un-tested backlog inventory.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Developers forced to subordinate to QA might feel underutilized or resist doing manual testing, causing morale friction.
2. **Data Gap:** Need post data comparing a 5 Focusing Steps narrative against standard Kanban WIP limit posts.
3. **Bias Check:** Ensure subordination does not become an excuse for senior developers to disengage or shirk complex architectural tasks.
