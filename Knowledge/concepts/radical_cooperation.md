# Radical Cooperation

## Definition
From Marcus Aurelius: "We were born to work together like feet, hands and eyes, like the two rows of teeth, upper and lower. To obstruct each other is unnatural."

## Current Rule
- This is the philosophical backing for **Cross-Functional Teams** and destroying silos.
- Use this in the **Manager Partnership** pillar to argue that "Vertical Slicing" isn't just an efficiency hack; it's a return to natural human cooperation over artificial corporate borders.

## Evolution
- **Apr 2026:** Ingested into Ledger to provide a human-centric argument for cross-functional teams.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps

1. **Against:** Radical cooperation can lead to groupthink, committee-based design, and dilute individual accountability. Solo deep work can be more efficient than constant cross-functional sync.
2. **Data Gap:** Cycle time and quality metrics comparing mob/pair programming vs. solo execution with clear boundaries.
3. **Bias Check:** Agile literature vilifies silos; must balance with organizational design theory showing the value of specialized functional groups for deep expertise.
