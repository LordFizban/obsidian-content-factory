# Systems Archetypes

## Definition
Generic structural patterns that recur across organizations, serving as diagnostic tools to uncover the root causes of systemic failures. Primary examples from Senge include *Limits to Growth*, *Shifting the Burden* (heroics replacing fundamental capability), and *Fixes that Fail*.

## Current Rule
**Rule 33 (Proposed):** Use *Shifting the Burden* to critique Scrum Master heroics: when an SM continuously steps in to patch sprint gaps, they create an addiction loop that degrades team self-organization.

## Evolution
- **2026-08-02:** Ingested from *The Fifth Discipline*. Mapped directly to the Brent Bottleneck and Facilitator Restraint concepts.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Structural archetypes can feel overly abstract or academic to frontline software engineers and non-executive readers.
2. **Data Gap:** Need post performance data testing an explicit archetype narrative (e.g. Shifting the Burden story) against a standard retro retrospective post.
3. **Bias Check:** We might fit every team problem into an archetype pattern even when the root cause is simple miscommunication or missing technical tools.
