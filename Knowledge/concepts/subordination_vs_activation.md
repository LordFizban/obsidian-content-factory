# Subordination vs. Activation (The Efficiency Trap)

## Definition
The operational principle that non-bottleneck resources must not be activated beyond the capacity of the system's primary constraint. Forcing 100% utilization on non-bottlenecks creates excess work-in-progress (WIP) inventory, inflates lead times, and degrades overall throughput.

## Current Rule
**Rule 37 (Proposed):** Resist 100% developer allocation demands from management. Non-bottlenecks require deliberate slack capacity to absorb variability and prevent flooding the bottleneck.

## Evolution
- **2026-08-02:** Ingested from Eliyahu Goldratt's *The Goal*. Selected as a primary concept for Manager Partnership content.

## Counter-Arguments & Data Gaps
1. **Against:** Middle managers conditioned on traditional cost accounting see idle non-bottleneck capacity as wasted salary, making subordination hard to sell without strong trust.
2. **Data Gap:** Need post performance data testing an explicit "100% utilization trap" hook against a standard capacity planning post.
3. **Bias Check:** Ensure slack capacity is used for learning, refactoring, or assisting bottlenecks, rather than passive downtime.
